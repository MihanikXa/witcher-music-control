## Questions to settle with evidence

**A. What exactly resets the rest?** On current approved-cue Sparse baseline, distinguish:
1. Reposting the same location event while selected location is already active vs changing location away and back.
2. Reapplying the same \`game_state=exploration\` vs a real day→night→day or exploration→dialogue→exploration transition.
3. Changes while the cue is still playing vs while in early/middle/near-end quiet rest.
4. Original selector event replay, State setting, and underlying native playlist reinitialization vs continuation — identify actual voice/segment IDs, accepted switch values, timeline and actual PCM. A pure source-level inference without capture is *not* an observation.
5. Changes that *should* discard the old musical selection (real region, quest, combat or authored branch changes) versus merely redundant updates, and whether the former can resume remaining quiet time on returning. Protect native selector authority: no indiscriminate "ignore all location changes" patch.

**B. What native behavior occurs after combat?** During cue, early rest, late rest, and again on rapid repeated combat entries, measure the order/timings of: accepted combat State, actual combat voice, threat intensity, native outro segment800050000, exploration selector re-entry, imported cue first audible sample and reverb/tail. Determine whether a quiet recovery can be inserted **after the complete outro** (or while it safely completes with generic cue still absent), without hiding or truncating the outro. Baseline report87 retains middle outro around -19dB in Sparse; the retained D replay previously attenuated its middle to -219dB. *No regression to the historical D defect is acceptable.*
