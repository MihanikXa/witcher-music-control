**B. Build a per-key three-way migration ledger**:
`resource/movie or script | original 0.8.0 | live customized owner and delta | 0.8.6 intermediate where relevant | new 0.8.7 upstream | keep / transfer / replace / hold | required tests`.
Classify: (i) unchanged upstream asset, (ii) upstream change to take, (iii) our authored modification to preserve/port, (iv) external functional mod contract, (v) real binary/script conflict requiring a combined component. Do not infer an asset is “vanilla” or safe from package names or load order alone. A copied 0.8.7 movie may erase old custom behavior even if it looks correct.
