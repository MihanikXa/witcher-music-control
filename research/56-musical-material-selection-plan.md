## Required process

1. **Index once, reuse prior work.** Read existing metadata and recover track/display names, origin/source, playlist context, Wwise segment/track/source IDs, WEM cache-entry ID/path and any layer/silent/duplicate status. Never infer complete cue identity from a file basename alone. Use actual payload metadata, map records, and flag broken/ambiguous joins. Separate native original files from the 275 extra Music Plus streams, and don't interpret number of entries as 275 independent compositions.
2. **Prioritize intelligently.** Inventory all practical candidates with duration, musical/reuse relationships, available playback markers and album/origin hints where present. Avoid making a 900MB copy per test or decoding every duplicate without reason. Filter out silence/test-only material and identify the multi-layer pieces that cannot stand alone.
3. **Listen to the music.** Use locally installed, trusted decoding/playback tools. Never execute Music Plus-supplied EXE/DLL/BAT/editor code just to audition. If decoding is blocked without an untrusted executable, document it and request a specific next action; don't pretend audio was inspected. If automated audio metrics are available, use them for duration, loudness envelope and boundary detection, not as proxies for musical quality, emotional suitability or satisfactory endings. If Codex cannot listen directly, clearly mark its listening judgments as **unverified** and make an audition pack for the user.
4. **Choose 15–25 distinct, traceable candidate musical gestures** for sparse exploration, not simply 15–25 short files. Prefer self-contained passages, natural entrances/exits, enough surrounding silence to matter, appropriate instrumental/atmospheric weight, restrained musical emphasis and meaningful differentiation. Include contrasting durations and some full pieces if their form genuinely suits the context. Evaluate whether pieces need natural Wwise segment/marker boundaries, permissible local trims/fades, or cannot be extracted without harming musical phrasing. Avoid arbitrary 20-second cuts, imposing exact emotional genre from names, or overusing important authored/thematic quest cues in generic exploration.
5. **Make an accessible, local audition deliverable.** Under a new ignored `build/music-material-selection/` directory, provide a reproducible CSV/JSON catalog and a concise HTML or M3U audition index with stable source IDs and durations, with locally generated private playback previews only where proper trusted decoding can be verified. For promising long pieces, surface representative entrance, middle and resolution regions (note that excerpts are convenience for listening, not authorization to splice them into the game). Keep the user's review friction low; a shortlist and listening order are more useful than a database alone. Never commit music data, previews or converted audio.
6. **Make calibrated recommendations.** Rank the most promising 5–8 first, plus the broader 15–25 audition list. For each provide: exact source ID/path and album/track label where grounded; duration; Wwise use and evidence; candidate in/out times or native exit markers if available; why plausible as punctuation vs background; risks of wrong context, incomplete phrase, mixing or original quest significance; confidence; and what requires actual human listening. Keep structural/musical inference separate from verified ear-tested findings.
7. **Conclude with a material strategy.** Recommend whether the promising pool is (a) mostly original native sources, (b) additional Music Plus pack recordings, or (c) a mixture. State whether self-contained short gestures exist in enough variety or whether editing/arrangement would be necessary. Do not begin integration, scheduler refinement or gameplay-trigger discovery in this task.
