## Proposed narrow exceptions, with no speculative shipping edit

For an approved village_q505 exception, protect only its **noncombat selection**589261176 and its mapped E/D/traversal contexts; keep its combat970815018/Gwent944324079 native. The existing five static D properties do not target this object, so they require no reversal. Winter/tavern exceptions likewise concern251819879/531434506, separately reviewed. Never exempt the entire region or automatically every quest-labelled location.

**Critical architecture correction to historical report07:** its OutputBus override bypassed a proposed bus mixer. Current Stage1 attenuation is **ancestor Voice Volume**, not a custom bus. Routing a child to Music is not demonstrated to remove that ancestor gain; do not implement this as a presumed exemption. Removing a child's static Volume alone also cannot remove the root State mute. A constant+200 dB compensation would incorrectly boost combat/unattenuated States and is rejected.

Smallest dependable design to validate next: keep original selector/event hierarchy but **move the existing contextual State policy from the shared world ancestor onto reviewed generic physical subtrees**, leaving the approved authored noncombat subtree without any added attenuating ancestor. Retain D static−200 on approved generic-only playlists to prevent continuation bursts; no new bus/global/RTPC/script needed. Before authoring, resolve actual parents and all cross-selector consumers: selector membership is not proof of gain ancestry. For White Orchard the q505 playlist has its own subtree; its combat/Gwent nodes remain separate. An implementation may scope StateInfo to the other reviewed location selectors or finer playlists, but must prove no excluded authored path inherits it and no generic path loses protection. This modifies more local State serialization than D; it needs a separate source/bank gate and native-playlist crossfade test, not a relaxed five-record gate.

This is a **proposed** reuse of demonstrated local State gains, not a validated exception build. Child State cancellation/DisableStates inheritance has not been established and is not relied on. Leaving uncertain selections native would favor story preservation but creates an explicit generic-suppression coverage gap; record that rather than presenting it as complete policy.
