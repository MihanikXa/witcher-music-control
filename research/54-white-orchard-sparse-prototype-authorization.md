# 54 — Authorized White Orchard sparse cadence experiment — 2026-10-16

## User authorization and intended scope

After reviewing actual Music Plus 3.3.0 code/banks and the comparison in report53, the user said: **"Sounds good to me, let's try that."** This authorizes one **isolated offline White Orchard experimental native-only sparse-rest prototype** to test whether occasional original-music playback followed by deliberate environmental rests can coexist with existing vanilla combat/quest transition grammar. It does **not** authorize deployment, retail-game launch, a full-region rollout, modifying the current v2 Vortex installation, changing v4, taking copyrighted Music Plus material, committing third-party assets or adopting particular defaults.

Goal: implement and **measure** one reviewed generic exploration selection playing existing native music through its musical exit marker, entering a modest bounded silent rest, then continuing safely. Combat and authored events must be able to interrupt both music and rest promptly with vanilla complete intro/intensity/outro behavior. This is a **feasibility experiment**, not the final Breath of the Wild/Ghost of Tsushima-inspired intentional-context scoring model. It should be reversible to the existing Minimal policy and isolated from protected world cues.

## Critical design constraint: D already has two mutes

The retained installed/verified White Orchard D is not a flat pristine bank: its existing 25-State world-music ancestor has exploration/dialogue suppression, and five approved generic playlists have static Voice Volume -200 dB (reports27–28,35). A naive rest scheduler under a static -200 dB playlist or inherited world E/D suppression will have no audible musical phrase. Conversely, globally removing world State suppression can unsafely reveal world-authored exceptions and other generic content.

**Require a concrete gain-ancestry solution in the experiment**, with separate controls for pristine vanilla, retained D Minimal, and the isolated candidate, proving both the audible music leg and silent rest leg. The new sparse path must be eligible only on one pinned, proven generic selection, without granting unintended gain to other world or authored descendants. New World State relocation/routing is *not* assumed safe; demonstrate bounded isolation, or stop with exact blocker. Retain the current D unchanged; do not call a candidate compatible with D unless its independent source/voice measurements show real compatibility. Reference report28's exception-isolation lesson and report35's safe static-only fixture.

## Technical acceptance gate

A passing experiment must establish **audible native generic phrase at ordinary gain → measured bounded quiet rest → viable next native phrase**, with **combat interrupting at both phases while its native intro/intensity/full outro remain audible**, and protected authored cues and Music master functioning normally. Explicitly measure whether day/night/location changes honor acceptable cue-boundary behavior.

If one mechanism breaks original cues or requires breaking native combat transitions, record the falsifying observation and try at most one clearly justified local alternative. Do not endlessly expand scope, attempt a full eight-region build or invent final timing/context policy.

User-facing conclusion: state whether the isolated mechanism worked, what native behavior changed (if any), whether the experiment genuinely composes with D / Minimal, and the smallest worthwhile next step toward *meaningful-context* punctuation.
