## Supported-tool attempts and remaining limitations

REDkit wcc's isolated runtime still cannot initialize against the incomplete depot (prior startup failure). Its standalone bundlebuilder help runs without a depot, but valid bundle-definition/export metadata was unavailable; private trials generated no supported new bundle. A .NET-built WolvenKit-7 CLI successfully runs, but all 18 competing resources are CR2W v164 and its reader accepts only 159–163. Export returned zero while logging UnsupportedVersion and generating no JSON: checked as failure. No header-version bypass or arbitrary byte editing was used. No supported alternate WitcherScript compiler pass was obtained. Runtime compilation remains the first personal acceptance test.
