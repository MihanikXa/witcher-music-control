Original design study · 9 October 2026

A quieter voice
for the Continent.

Three literary directions. These are browser mockups on synthetic backgrounds, not screenshots or validated Scaleform output. Size, shadow and font shaping here are comparison aids.

Checking local preview fonts…

01 · Quiet Editorial

Gentium Book throughout. Warm, coherent and closest to the reference; small controls need careful width and weight checks.

02 · Field & Folio Preferred

Gentium Book for names, speech and reading. Source Sans 3 for controls and numbers. Literary warmth with a clearer practical hierarchy.

03 · Humanist Chronicle

Alegreya for names and reading, Source Sans 3 for controls. More rhythmic texture; evaluate figures and wider letterforms before adoption.

No option assumes that changing the three global font aliases can assign a different family to each UI role. Option 02/03 require an additional embedded family and targeted fields; this is a later implementation gate. The first game proof keeps the current font to isolate color and shadow.

The soft shadow alone fails against uniform snow and bright sky. Compare the backing mode before choosing a comfort preset. Hostility also retains its health bar, target indicators and game behavior. This mockup does not simulate HDR, motion, color blindness or controller distance.

Preview sources: SIL Gentium Book 7.000, upstream Alegreya and Source Sans 3 (OFL 1.1). All binaries remain under ignored build/. Design specification.
